## Wu Jen

Wu Jen 法术规划表按 1–9 级列出法术，并使用 C/R/D/A/S 栏记录施法时间、射程、持续时间、作用范围和豁免。低等级法术包括 Accuracy、Animate Wood、Chameleon、Cloud Ladder、Comprehend Languages、Detect Magic、Elemental Burst、Fiery Eyes、Ghostlight、Hail of Stone、Hold Portal、Hypnotism、Know History、Magic Missile、Melt、Message、Prestidigitation、Read Magic、Secret Signs、Shield、Spider Climb、Still Water、Swim、Unseen Servant、Ventriloquism、Wall of Fog、Warp Wood、Water Protection 和 Wizard Mark。

中高等级栏包含 Animal Companion、Animate Water、Apparition、Bind、Detect Evil、Detect Invisibility、Enchanted Blade、ESP、Fire Shuriken、Fog Cloud、Ice Knife、Invisibility、Knock、Locate Object、Animate Fire、Cloudburst、Commune with Lesser Spirit、Detect Shapechanger、Disguise、Dispel Magic、Fabricate、Feign Death、Fire Rain、Fire Wings、Haste、Hold Person、Bargain、Confusion、Dancing Blade、Dimension Door、Dispel Illusion、Dream Vision、Elemental Turning、Emotion、Improved Invisibility、Melt Metal、Minor Creation、Plant Growth、Polymorph Other、Polymorph Self、Remove Curse、Shout、Spectral Force、Wall of Fire 等法术。

高等级栏收录 Control Weather、Geas、Glassee、Lower Water、Move Earth、Body Outside Body、Commune with Greater Spirit、Limited Wish、Antipathy/Sympathy、Call、Incendiary Cloud、Astral Spell、Crystalbrittle、Instant Regeneration、Internal Fire、Aiming at the Target、Animal Growth、Creeping Darkness、Dismissal、Metal Skin、Aura、Pain、Part Water、Permanent Illusion、Spiritwrack、Stone to Flesh、Power Word: Stun、Withering Palm、Mind Blank、Permanency、Polymorph Any Object、Symbol、Whirlwind、Planar Call、Power Word: Kill、Tsunami 和 Wish。

## Shukenja

Shukenja 表按 1–7 级列出东方牧师法术，并保留正常/反转法术成对排列，例如 Cure Serious Wounds/Cause Serious Wounds、Protection from Evil/Protection from Good、Neutralize Poison/Poison、Detect Lie/Undetectable Lie、True Seeing/False Seeing、Heal/Harm、Raise Dead/Slay Living、Resurrection/Destruction。
